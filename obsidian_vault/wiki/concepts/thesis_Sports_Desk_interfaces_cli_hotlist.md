## What It Does Not Do

It does not place a bet, and it does not rank by edge alone - it ranks by APPROVED DOLLARS, because a 40% edge the bankroll gate sizes to $12 is worth less attention than a 30% edge it sizes to $300. Nothing here writes to the ledger; the tax record is made when the wager is actually placed and imported through `Tax_Reserve_Agent`.
