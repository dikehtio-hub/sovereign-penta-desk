6. EMERGENCY BRAKES, FAIL-SAFE SENTINELS & RULES
================================================================================

1. NEVER-SEED LIVE LEDGER RULE:
   `python -m Tax_Reserve_Agent.main seed-bankroll` writes a synthetic DEPOSIT row.
   NEVER run this on the live production ledger. On live accounts, real bankroll
   must only be established by importing real deposit receipts from CSVs.

2. FAIL-CLOSED ON UNKNOWN DEX:
   Only DEXes explicitly listed in `CRYPTO_DEXES` (`main`) or `MIXED_DEXES` (`para`
   under allow-list) can trade basis perps. Novel DEXes discovered live fail closed
   by default and trigger an amber alert in the dashboard.

3. SPREAD & LIQUIDITY ENTRY GATES:
   - Gross APR >= 25.0%
   - Net APR >= 20.0% (after spread amortization & fees)
   - Max Top-of-Book Spread <= 25.0 bps
   - Spot Pair 24h Volume >= $100,000.00 (10x notional)

4. RESTART COLLECTOR ON SETTINGS CHANGES:
   Python settings are cached on import. Any change to `config/settings.py` requires
   stopping and starting the collector via `stop_collector.bat` then `start_collector.bat`.
